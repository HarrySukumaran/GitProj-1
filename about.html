About This Site

A tiny corner of the web, built for the love of learning.

This little site started as a single "Hello, World!" page — the classic first step for anyone learning the web. From there it grew: a shared stylesheet, a navigation bar, and pages that actually talk to each other.

Everything here is hand-crafted with plain HTML and CSS. No frameworks, no build steps — just markup, styles, and a few well-placed animations. Open the source in your editor and poke around; that's the best way to learn.
